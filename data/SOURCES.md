These flags answer “if you already have nexus, is this SKU even taxable?” They are **not** statutes. DC is not in that table; the desk marks DC digital flags as confirm-with-OTR.

## What this desk does not do

- Physical-nexus (inventory, reps, servers).
- Marketplace-facilitator collection vs seller collection (it only flags whether marketplace volume **counts toward the threshold**).
- Local / home-rule (CO, AK, AL, etc.).
- Filing calendars, rates, or registrations.
